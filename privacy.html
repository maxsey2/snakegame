Privacy

SnakeForFree.com has no accounts. Audience statistics are only collected if you accept them.

What is stored

Your best scores, your last-used mode and your cookie choice are saved in your browser's local storage. They never leave your device.

Cookies and analytics

With your consent, we use Google Analytics, a service of Google, to understand how the site is used: pages viewed, approximate location, device type and game events such as the number of games played and scores. It sets cookies named _ga and _ga_ followed by an identifier, typically kept for up to two years. Nothing is loaded and no analytics cookie is set until you click Accept.

You can change your choice at any time with the "Cookie settings" link in the footer. Choosing Decline removes the analytics cookies from this browser. Your choice is remembered for about six months.

Google may process this data outside the European Economic Area; see Google's own privacy policy for details. Fonts are also loaded from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when you visit.

Deleting your data

Clear this site's data in your browser settings to remove your scores.
